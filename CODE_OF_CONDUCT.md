# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community.

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, and
avoid personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized content, threats,
or publishing private information. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and restrict participation when conduct is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
